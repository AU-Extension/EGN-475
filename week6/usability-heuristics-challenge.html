Usability heuristics challenge

A prototype that works can still confuse people. Each screen below comes from a café or salon app and has one usability problem. Find it, name the heuristic it breaks, and pick the fix that solves it. These are the same problems your usability test in Assignment 6.2 should uncover.

How to use this challenge (and the ten heuristics)
  1. Read the situation, then click the part of the screen that causes the problem (3 points).
  2. Choose the heuristic it breaks (4 points).
  3. Choose the best fix (3 points) and read the explanation.
  4. Continue through all ten screens for a total of 100 points.
