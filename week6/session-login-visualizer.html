Sessions and login: what really happens

Register an account, log in, and visit pages that need a login or a staff role. Watch the password become a hash, the session cookie arrive in the browser, and the server check it on every request. Then try to cheat by editing the cookie.

How to use this interactive
  1. Choose your project option. The pages and route names change to match.
  2. Register a new account, then log in with it. Watch the users table and the cookie jar.
  3. Use the address buttons under the browser to visit the protected pages as a guest, as a customer or client, and as staff (a staff account is already in the database).
  4. While logged in as a customer or client, press "Edit the cookie to say staff" and try the staff page again.
  5. Finish with the quick check at the bottom.
