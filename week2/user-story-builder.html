User story builder

A user story says who needs something, what they need and why. Build stories for your café or salon app, add acceptance criteria, and let the INVEST checker tell you what to improve before you hand the story to Copilot.

How to use this interactive
  1. Load an example story, or start writing your own in the three fields.
  2. Add at least two acceptance criteria in Given / When / Then form.
  3. Watch the INVEST checker on the right and fix every red or amber item.
  4. Add the finished story to your backlog. Repeat for more stories.
  5. Export the backlog as Markdown and paste it into USER_STORIES.md for Assignment 2.1.
