Follow one feature from a pencil sketch to a working app. At each level, see what it costs to build, what it can teach you, and when it is the right choice for a client.

How to use this interactive
  1. Pick a client: Café Calle Ocho (place an order) or Bella Vista Salon (book an appointment).
  2. Move through the four fidelity levels with the buttons or the slider.
  3. Try the preview at each level. From the clickable mockup on, you can actually click.
  4. Read the facts card: time, cost of change, what you learn, and when to use it.
  5. Finish with the "which fidelity fits?" check at the bottom.

Throwaway or evolutionary?

Throwaway prototype

Built only to learn, then discarded. Paper sketches, wireframes and most clickable mockups are throwaway: you keep the lessons, not the artifact. They are cheap, so throwing them away is fine.

Evolutionary prototype

Built to grow into the real product. The Flask app you build in this course is evolutionary: every week you add one working feature to the same code, and by Week 8 it is deployed.
