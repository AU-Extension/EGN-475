Demo day readiness challenge

Five minutes is enough to show a prototype well, if you plan them. Build the outline of a strong demo, pick the better opening lines, spot weak claims, and finish with a checklist you can print and bring to class.

How to use this interactive
  1. Part 1 (25 points): put the parts of a five-minute demo in order with the arrow buttons, then press Check.
  2. Part 2 (25 points): for each pair, choose the stronger thing to say.
  3. Part 3 (30 points): mark each claim or slide as strong or weak.
  4. Part 4 (20 points): tick only what is really true for your project today. This part is a self-assessment.
  5. Press Print my readiness summary to save a page with your score, your outline, and your remaining to-do items.
